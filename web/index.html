Severe blood pressure & possible hypertensive emergency

A transparent adult screening example based on the 2025 AHA/ACC guideline. No medication selection or BP-reduction targets are calculated.

Not a diagnostic or treatment device. Blood pressure alone cannot diagnose or exclude acute target-organ injury. If there is chest pain, severe shortness of breath, new neurological deficit, confusion, seizure, or other concerning acute symptoms, seek emergency care irrespective of this tool's output.

Concerning acute symptoms or suspected target-organ injury?

A symptom report does not establish organ damage; absent symptoms do not exclude it.

Pregnancy, postpartum hypertensive disorders and stroke require separate condition-specific evaluation.

Interpretation limits

  • Severe threshold: systolic >180 or diastolic >120 mm Hg (AHA/ACC 2025).
  • Hypertensive emergency: severe elevation with evidence of acute target-organ damage, which requires clinical assessment.
  • Without acute damage: aggressive short-term BP reduction is generally not indicated solely from the measurement.
  • Stroke, acute aortic syndromes and pregnancy have distinct clinical pathways.

References

2025 AHA/ACC high blood pressure guideline, §6.2; 2024 AHA acute-care scientific statement; 2024 ESC hypertension guideline.
